Task 5 - Complexity Analysis
Measured metric: comparisons during insertion
Scenarios:
1) BST with sorted input (worst case)
2) BST with random input (average case)
3) AVL with sorted input (self-balancing)

Interpretation:
- BST(sorted) grows approximately quadratically over full build (sum of linear path lengths).
- BST(random) grows slower, close to n*log(n) behavior in practice.
- AVL(sorted) also stays near n*log(n) due to balancing rotations.
